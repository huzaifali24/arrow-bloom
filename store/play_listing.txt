Full description (max 4000)
---------------------------
Every arrow wants out. Only some of them can go.

Arrow Bloom is a clean, relaxing logic puzzle with a sharp twist. Tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points, but only if its path is clear. Find the right order, clear every arrow and solve the level.

Easy to learn, hard to master. The first boards take seconds. The big ones will make you stop, look twice and plan your way out.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap an arrow to send it off the board
• An arrow can only leave if nothing blocks its path
• Hitting another arrow costs a heart, so look before you tap
• Clear the whole board to win

SIX DIFFICULTY LEVELS
Normal, Medium, Hard, Expert, Master and Insane. Boards grow bigger and more tangled as you go, with hundreds of arrows locked together on the toughest levels. Harder levels pay more coins.
